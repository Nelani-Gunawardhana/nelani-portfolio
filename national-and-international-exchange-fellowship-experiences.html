May – June 2026 · In person USA Workshop

Young South Asian Leaders Initiative (YSALI) Workshop on American Excellence, Entrepreneurship, and Innovation

Institution / Host: U.S. Department of State, University of Nebraska at Omaha and East West Center

Year / Period: May – June 2026

Status / Role: Selected Participant to Represent Sri Lanka

The Young South Asian Leaders Initiative (YSALI) U.S. Summer Workshop is an immersive two-week exchange program designed to connect nearly 80 emerging leaders from across South Asia with the U.S. spirit of entrepreneurship and innovation. Jointly organized by the University of Nebraska at Omaha's Center for Afghanistan and Regional Studies, the East-West Center, and the U.S. Department of State, the program brings together young professionals from nations like Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Maldives, Nepal, Pakistan, and Sri Lanka. Through visits to leading businesses, government institutions, and innovation hubs in Omaha and Washington, D.C., participants engage with mentors, refine their leadership styles using tools like the Gallup CliftonStrengths assessment, and develop collaborative Action Plans to address critical community challenges in education, economic growth, and youth empowerment back home.
